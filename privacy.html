Privacy policy

Effective date: 22 October 2026.

Scope and operator

This notice applies to the personal Google Workspace integration named Hermes, operated by the owner of the Estogen GitHub account. It is a personal automation tool, not a public service. For questions or deletion requests, contact the operator through the repository's issue tracker. Do not post private information, tokens, or spreadsheet contents there.

Data accessed and purpose

The integration requests the Google Sheets OAuth scope to read spreadsheet content and update spreadsheets as instructed by the authorized account owner. This can include financial records, dates, descriptions, formulas, and spreadsheet identifiers. This scope is not limited by Google to one individual spreadsheet; operations are selected according to the owner's instructions.

Processing and sharing

Selected spreadsheet data may be included in the owner's Hermes Agent conversation and processed by the AI provider configured for that installation to fulfill the owner's requests. The operator controls that provider configuration. The integration does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Google API data is used for the requested assistance, not to build a generalized model. Providers' applicable terms and the owner's account settings govern their processing.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials are stored in local credential files on the operator's machine or server; they are not intentionally published in this repository or website. Task inputs and results may be retained in local conversation logs, operational files, and the owner's notes. Google Sheets retains the spreadsheets and their revision history. Retention is controlled by the operator; no automatic deletion period is promised.

Control and deletion

The account owner can revoke access at Google Account connections. Revocation prevents further authorized API access but does not delete existing spreadsheet records, notes, or logs. The operator can remove locally stored credentials and task records. Google-hosted records can be deleted through Google Sheets and the owner's Google Account controls.

Website

These static information pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. No application analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, or credential collection forms are included. GitHub may process technical request data under its own privacy statement.

Google API Limited Use

The integration's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
